# WarBreach

A landscape base-defense game for phones. Build and upgrade a military base, then survive endless waves of enemy armies.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser, or host the folder on any static host (GitHub Pages works). On a phone, use **Add to Home Screen** for a fullscreen landscape app. Progress saves in the browser automatically.

## Features

- Top-down military compound with fence, gates and approach roads
- Command Center (HQ levels 1–5) that unlocks buildings, storage and higher levels
- Economy: Gold Mine, Steel Foundry, Supply Depot (tap bubbles to collect)
- Defenses: Machine Gun Nest, Cannon, Mortar Pit, Sniper Tower, Missile Battery
- Walls in five tiers that link together as you lay them
- Build timers with 2 builders, upgrade / move / sell, offline production
- Touch controls: drag to pan, pinch to zoom, drag the outline to place
- Battle mode: endless waves come down the west, east and north roads
  - Riflemen, Grenadiers, Armored Jeeps, Light Tanks and Attack Drones
  - Enemies path around walls when they can and break through when they can't
  - Each defense fires its own way: MG tracers, cannon shells, lobbed mortars, sniper shots, homing rockets
  - The Command Center has a rooftop gun
  - Kills and cleared waves pay gold and steel; damage is repaired after the battle
  - Speed x1/x2/x3, retreat, best-wave record

Flame Trooper (burns buildings) · Heavy Gunner (armor shrugs off light bullets) ·

## Update 6: ideas from the genre's biggest hits

Researched Kingdom Rush, Bloons TD 6, Boom Beach and Clash of Clans, and added the systems that keep players coming back:

- **Heroes** (Kingdom Rush): a commander unit joins every battle. Tap the map to move them; enemies stop to fight them. Three heroes (Commando, Field Engineer, Heavy Weapons), each with a special ability, levels 1-10.
- **Targeting priority** (Bloons TD 6): set each defense to shoot the Closest, First, Strongest or Weakest enemy.
- **Specializations** (Kingdom Rush): at level 5 a defense picks Rapid Fire, Heavy Rounds or Long Barrel.
- **Research Lab** (Clash of Clans / Boom Beach): permanent base-wide upgrades such as damage, hit points, production, airstrikes, build speed and repairs.
- **Daily challenge** (Bloons TD 6): a new five-wave challenge every day with two random rule changes and a medal reward.
- **Daily supply** (Boom Beach): a 7-day login reward calendar.
- **Next-wave preview and call early** (Kingdom Rush): see what's coming and start the next wave early for bonus gold.
- **First-time tutorial**: guided hints for new players.
- **Service record**: lifetime stats from the menu.

## Code layout

- `js/data.js` – building stats, costs, limits per HQ level, wave previews
- `js/render.js` – procedural terrain and building art (canvas)
- `js/audio.js` – synthesized sound effects and vibration
- `js/army.js` – Barracks training, troop deployment, troop AI, rally and reinforcements
- `js/arsenal.js` – abilities, loadout and their battle effects
- `js/raid.js` – the weekly Behemoth boss raid
- `js/meta.js` – War Chest, achievements, Quartermaster, obstacles, themes, backups, what's new
- `js/frontline.js` – Update 2 systems: wave upgrades, factions, Workshop, daily orders, events, Wardrobe, layouts, surprise attacks, Firing Range, world map, Operations hub
- `js/hero.js` – heroes: movement, combat, abilities, levels and the hero screen
- `js/campaign.js` – campaign missions: operation map, briefings, results and stars
- `js/battle.js` – battle mode: waves, enemy pathing (flow field), turret fire, rewards
- `js/game.js` – state, saving, placement, input, HUD and main loop
- `style.css` – HUD, panels, shop and modals
